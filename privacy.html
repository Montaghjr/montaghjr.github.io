Privacy policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Gizmo is a voice assistant that one family runs on its own computer at home. It is not a public service. Only members of that household connect their accounts to it.

What Gizmo accesses from Google

When a household member connects a Google account, Gizmo asks for two permissions:

  • See the list of calendars on the account (calendar.calendarlist.readonly).
  • View and edit events on those calendars (calendar.events).

Gizmo asks for nothing else. It does not access email, contacts, files or photos.

How Gizmo uses it

  • To answer spoken questions about the family schedule.
  • To add an event when a household member asks out loud. Gizmo reads the event back and writes it only after a yes.

Gizmo does not use calendar data for advertising or profiling. It does not use calendar data to train AI models.

Where the data is kept

  • Sign-in tokens are stored on the household computer. They live in the macOS Keychain or in a private file that only the computer's owner can read.
  • Calendar events are fetched from Google when they are needed. Gizmo does not save them.
  • Gizmo keeps a log of spoken requests on the same computer for troubleshooting. Its spoken calendar answers are left out of that log. A request itself can mention a calendar event.
  • Requests are handled by an AI model that runs on the household computer itself. Gizmo is set up so calendar data does not go to any outside AI service.

Sharing

Gizmo does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. Nobody outside the household has access to it.

Limited Use

Gizmo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect Gizmo at any time. Go to myaccount.google.com/connections and remove Gizmo. After that it can no longer read or change your calendar. The household can also delete your stored sign-in from the computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact
