Gizmo

A voice assistant for one family's kitchen.

Gizmo runs on a computer in our home. You talk to it and it talks back. It is a private family project. It is not sold or offered to the public, and there is no way to sign up.

What it does

  • Keeps the family shopping lists and pantry up to date by voice.
  • Reads recipes step by step and runs kitchen timers.
  • Answers questions about the weather.
  • Reads the family's Google Calendars out loud. "What's on tomorrow?" works.
  • Adds an event to a family member's calendar when they ask. It reads the event back first and waits for a yes.

Your Google account

Family members can connect their own Google account so Gizmo can read and add calendar events. Gizmo asks for calendar access only. It does not ask for email, contacts or files. The privacy policy explains what Gizmo does with that access and how to remove it.
